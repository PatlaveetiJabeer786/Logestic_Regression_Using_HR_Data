## 🔴 Business Problem  

Organizations often need to classify outcomes into **binary categories** (Yes/No, 0/1, Success/Failure).  
Without predictive modeling:  
- ❌ Decisions rely on guesswork  
- ❌ No framework to estimate probabilities  
- ❌ Business risks increase due to uncertainty  

➡️ The goal is to apply **Logistic Regression** to predict categorical outcomes and demonstrate its use in real‑world classification problems.

## 📈 Results  

- ✅ **Model Accuracy** → Achieved ~82% accuracy on test data  
- ✅ **Precision & Recall** → Balanced performance across both classes  
- ✅ **ROC Curve** → AUC score ~0.87, showing strong classification ability  
- ✅ **Interpretability** → Coefficients revealed feature impact on outcome
